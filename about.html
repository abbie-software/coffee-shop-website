Small-batch beans, big-hearted hospitality.

Our Story

Abbie's Coffee House started with a simple idea: a cozy corner where friends, neighbors, and travelers could slow down, sip something delicious, and feel right at home. What began as a tiny espresso cart has grown into a warm, welcoming space filled with the aroma of freshly roasted beans and the sound of easy conversation.

Our Coffee

We partner with small, sustainable farms that care for their land, their communities, and their craft. Every batch is roasted with care to bring out rich, nuanced flavors, whether you love a bold espresso shot or a silky-smooth latte.

What We Believe In

  • Community first – a place where everyone feels welcome.
  • Quality in every cup – from bean selection to final pour.
  • Sustainability – thoughtful sourcing and eco-friendly choices.

Visit Us

Whether you are grabbing a quick morning brew or settling in with a book, we are here to make your day a little warmer. Stop by, say hello, and let us pour you your new favorite cup.
